Methodology

How This Portal Works

Every data point on this portal originates from a formal RTI application filed under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

01

RTI Filed

A formal RTI application is submitted to the relevant Public Information Officer. Filing date and reference number are recorded immediately.

02

30-Day Clock

The statutory response deadline (30 days under RTI Act) is tracked publicly. Non-response itself becomes data.

03

Data Published

On receipt of response, data is extracted, verified, and published on the dashboard. Partial responses are noted.

04

Pattern Emerges

Across RTI cycles, systemic delays become visible as patterns — not isolated incidents, but institutional behaviour.
